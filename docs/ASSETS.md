# Characters and animals — choose one option (prompt 11)

Today the game uses **stylised animated-film figures built in code** (D25: workers, elephants, zebu carts, rafts) with simple GPU walk/haul/hammer motion. They are instanced, light on the laptop and need no license. Real rigged models would add proper skeletal animation (rope pulling, lifting). Pick one option below; I then import it as glTF with the same instancing so performance stays inside the Low preset.

| Option                                | What you get                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | License                                                                                                                                                                               | Watch out                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A. Quaternius packs** (recommended) | [Ultimate Animated Character Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatedanimatedcharacter.html): 50+ low-poly people with animations. [Ultimate Animated Animal Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html): 12 animals, walk/gallop animations, glTF available. | CC0 — free for commercial use, no credit required                                                                                                                                     | The animal list is not published on the page: check it contains an **elephant** and an **ox/cow** before we commit. People are modern/fantasy styles; we recolour clothing to Khmer sampot colours in code. |
| **B. Kenney Animated Characters**     | [Animated Characters 3](https://kenney.nl/assets/animated-characters-3): simple rigged people with walk/run/idle, easy to reskin                                                                                                                                                       | CC0                                                                                                                                                                                   | Very plain style; no animals — elephants and oxen would stay placeholders or come from option A / Poly Pizza.                                                                                               |
| **C. Mixamo**                         | Rig any character and pick from thousands of motions (hauling, pulling rope, hammering)                                                                                                                                                                                                | Free with an Adobe account; Adobe allows use in commercial games (see [Adobe community answer](https://community.adobe.com/questions-696/commercial-use-of-mixamo-characters-589418)) | You download the files yourself (needs your Adobe login). Characters must be exported per animation; larger files. No animals.                                                                              |

Also useful for single animals: [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza/bundle/Animated-Animal-Pack-ILAPXeUYiS) lists free low-poly models — **each model has its own license (CC0 or CC-BY)**; CC-BY needs a credit line on stream or in the description.

**My recommendation:** A for people and animals, with C only if you want very specific motions (rope pulling, stone lifting) later.

Reply with **A**, **B** or **C**. Nothing is downloaded until you choose.
